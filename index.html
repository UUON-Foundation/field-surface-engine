System architecture
Taylor series e^z in the complex plane, evaluated per surface point, drives Euclidean geometric morphisms applied independently to four walls of a perspectively receding infinity tunnel. Each wall carries: shift, bevel, shadow, pivot blinds, and checkerboard — all from the same mathematical framework.
